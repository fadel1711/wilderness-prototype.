Your survival, farming, combat, exploration, crafting and save systems stay intact.

iPhone: Tap Bag → Claim free sword + shield, then equip Sword. Use Attack to strike when close and facing a wolf, hold Block when it bites, and tap Dodge to avoid damage. Watch health (HP) and stamina (SP). Rest near a bed or return home to recover health. Wolves remain outside your homestead zone and cannot damage structures. Tap axe/pickaxe to return to gathering. All original build and save features remain.
Survival: Watch the food, water and energy bars. Tap edible items in Bag to eat or drink; walk to the little spring southwest of home and tap Drink to refill water. Build or use a bed to sleep until morning. Switch off needs in Bag → Game settings using Creative Comfort. No offline penalties.
Two thumbs: Keep one thumb on the left joystick while tapping Jump, Gather, Attack or Dodge with the right. Hold Block with the right while moving with the left. Tap Jump near the right-hand action controls or press Space. Gravity, raised cabins and steps remain accessible.
Farming: Build a planter, walk up to it and tap Use to plant carrots, potatoes, or wheat using seeds you forage from wild patches marked S on the Map. When crops finish growing, harvest them for ingredients and seeds. Peaceful chickens and rabbits now wander the homestead. Furnace crafting includes a simple vegetable stew for recovery.
Map: Tap 🗺 Map to find your position, choose waypoints, and track discovered places. Open treasure chests when you reach them. Claim the Forgotten Camp cache to unlock the Ranger Rest blueprint.
Keyboard: WASD move · Space jump · M map · Shift jog · Drag camera · 1/2 tools · 3 sword · X attack · hold Q block · C dodge · E gather / Use · I Bag · B build. Your original save is kept separately. Tap + or − to zoom the view; scroll the mouse wheel on desktop.
