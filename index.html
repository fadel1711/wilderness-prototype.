YOUR FIRST HOMESTEAD

Wilderness 🌲

Explore a small 3D clearing, gather resources and build a cabin—then add your own floors and walls. This is an early playable mechanics test, not the final art style.

On iPhone: Left joystick to move, swipe the right side of the world to look, Gather near a resource, and Build to place a structure.

On computer: WASD / arrows to move, drag the mouse to look, E to gather, B to build, and F to place.

Progress saves to this browser, not to a server. Don't clear Safari website data if you want to keep it.
