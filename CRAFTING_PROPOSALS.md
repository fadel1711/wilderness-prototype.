# Wilderness — proposed Minecraft-inspired materials & crafts

**Proposal only. Not implemented in v0.23.1.** These should be approved before new item IDs or save-schema changes. The goal is to borrow Minecraft's clear gather → refine → craft → build loop, not its branding, creatures or exact world design.

## New materials (approval requested)

| Material | Obtain from | Proposed purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Clay | Creek and pond banks | Bricks, flowerpots, decorative roofs |
| Sand | River shores and sandy clearings | Glass panes for house windows |
| Flax / plant fiber | Wild grass patches and farming | Rope, cloth, backpacks |
| Copper ore | Rare surface rocks and mineable cave veins | Lanterns, metal decoration, cookware |
| Resin | Select trees, requires tapping tool | Torches, weatherproof building finishes |
| Leather | Future wildlife encounters or village trading | Better backpacks and clothing |
| Bones | Future night enemies and rare cave finds | Dog-taming treats in v0.25 |
| Gems | Rare deep-cave nodes | Trade valuables, cosmetic trinkets |

## Crafting recipes to consider (not final economy numbers)

| Craft | Suggested inputs | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Planks ×4 | 2 wood | Refined building material |
| Rope ×2 | 4 plant fiber | Construction & survival component |
| Glass panes ×2 | 3 sand + furnace fuel | Windows and lantern components |
| Brick block ×2 | 4 clay + furnace fuel | New wall/floor aesthetic |
| Reinforced door | 4 planks + 1 iron ingot | House upgrade / stronger doors |
| Lantern | 1 copper ingot + 1 glass pane + 1 charcoal | Light sources for cabins |
| Backpack upgrade | 2 leather + 2 rope | +8 inventory slots, one-time per player |
| Traveler bed | 4 planks + 3 cloth | Sleep while exploring, with safe placement rules |
| Storage crate | 8 planks | Smaller 8-slot container |
| Dog treat | 1 bone + 1 cooked meat | v0.25 taming consumable |

## Recommended order after approval

1. **Basic materials**: clay, sand, flax + planks, rope, glass, bricks.
2. **Crafting equipment & furniture**: upgraded doors, lanterns, storage, bed, backpack.
3. **Exploration economy**: copper, resin, gems and distinct village demand/supply.
4. **Companions & advanced survival**: bones, meat, tameable dog after v0.25.

Decisions needed: approve/reject each new material, whether backpacks should add slots, and whether village trades should use **barter only** or introduce a **non-premium in-game currency** later. Avoid implementing all of this in a hotfix: it increases inventory complexity, cloud-save requirements and balancing work.
